As the Creative Director for this luxury sales centre in Ras Al Khaimah, I envisioned a space that transcends traditional real estate showcases. The design celebrates coastal living through flowing architectural narratives, creating intimate moments—from the dramatic entrance ramp to private majlis areas overlooking the Arabian Gulf. Every material choice, from natural stone to ambient lighting, was curated to evoke an emotional connection with prospective buyers.
